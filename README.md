# ⚔️ My Favorite Anime: Attack on Titan

**Attack on Titan** is a Japanese anime about humanity fighting for survival against giant humanoid creatures called Titans.

## 📖 Plot
The story follows **Eren Yeager**, who joins the military to fight the Titans after his hometown is destroyed.

### ⭐ Why I Like It
- Amazing action and battles
- Unexpected plot twists
- Interesting characters
- Deep themes about **freedom and survival**

> "If you don't fight, you can't win."

## 🎬 Final Thoughts
*Attack on Titan* is one of my favorite anime because of its intense story, emotional moments, and shocking revelations.
